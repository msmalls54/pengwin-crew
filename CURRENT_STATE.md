## Research and integration limits

The shared Brave Web/Place service is implemented with an atomic 200-search cap and transient result handling, but no Brave API key is configured. The adapter now rejects private street addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses before making a provider request or reserving search budget, and handles empty provider sections. All four roles must say live search is unavailable in this deployment. The event plan has no validated city field, so Place queries remain geographically unanchored. Printful product prices were read from its official page as a product-only range. On September 27 the official Transbay Joint Powers Authority permits page and FAQ were checked for Salesforce Park: https://www.tjpa.org/permits-reservations and https://www.tjpa.org/salesforce-transit-center/faq . Events now returns the official inquiry route for Salesforce Park and labels availability UNCHECKED; it does not establish the correct permit category or a reservation.

The personal DoorDash CLI is excluded from Pengwin business buying under the documented account/license restriction in docs/orderly-doordash-reuse.md. The supplied residential address remains private and is not a demo default. No Printful store token, artwork, exact quote, or business checkout provider is configured. No attendee list was imported; no invitations were sent. OpenTable booking APIs require partner access. A provider receipt is required before claiming a venue reservation.

The previous Eventbrite free ticket publication and Airwallex sandbox transfer receipts remain in docs/eventbrite-integration.md and docs/airwallex-sandbox-receipt.md. Airwallex submission was read back as PROCESSING, not settled money. The workers still share one database login, so role separation is application-enforced; this needs stronger DB grants for real users. The judge token cannot access operator controls. The anonymous feed exposes only fixed role/action/status labels, checked product estimate, and aggregate counters; tests and deployment checks exclude Slack text, contact details, addresses, credentials, and provider references.

The public feed currently aggregates fictional demo rows from one database. Its new `PUBLIC_JUDGE_FEED` switch defaults off on fresh deployments; the hackathon control host explicitly enables it. Before real users or tenant data share this database, disable the endpoint or enforce tenant/demo scoping rather than exposing aggregate amounts and action times.
